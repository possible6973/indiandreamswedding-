Frequently Asked Questions — Wedding Planner in Surat

What types of events do you organize in Surat?

Indians Dream Wedding specializes in weddings, engagement ceremonies, haldi & sangeet functions, reception stages, baby showers, carnival themes, varmala setups, and corporate events. We operate across Surat, Ahmedabad, and all major cities in Gujarat.

How far in advance should we book your services?

Booking at least 1 month in advance is required (1 month pehle book karna zaroori hai). This ensures that our coordination teams, customized props, and designers are fully reserved for your special day.

Do you offer custom decoration themes?

Absolutely! Every event we design is fully customized to your vision. Whether you want a Royal Rajwadi theme, a modern minimalist setup, or a specific color palette — our team creates bespoke layouts tailored to your taste and budget.

What is included in your wedding packages?

We offer three wedding packages: Silver (₹2,00,000 for 100-200 guests), Gold (₹3,50,000 for 200-400 guests), and Platinum (₹5,00,000 for 300-600 guests). Features range from basic stage decoration to full wedding coordination, live music, and reel coverage. All packages can be customized.

Do you provide catering and entertainment services?

Yes, we offer complete event services. Catering options are customizable and can be added according to your selected package (Silver, Gold, or Platinum). We also arrange DJs, professional anchors, dance groups, live singers, and royal entries.
