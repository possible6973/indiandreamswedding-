Frequently Asked Questions

What types of events do you organize in Surat?

Indians Dream Wedding specializes in weddings, engagement ceremonies, haldi & sangeet functions, reception stages, baby showers, carnival themes, varmala setups, and corporate events. We operate across Surat, Ahmedabad, and all major cities in Gujarat.

How far in advance should we book your services?

We recommend booking at least 2–3 months in advance for weddings and large events to ensure your preferred dates and themes are available. For smaller events like baby showers or birthdays, 3–4 weeks is usually sufficient.

Do you offer custom decoration themes?

Absolutely! Every event we design is fully customized to your vision. Whether you want a Royal Rajwadi theme, a modern minimalist setup, or a specific color palette — our team creates bespoke layouts tailored to your taste and budget.

What is included in your wedding packages?

Our packages range from ₹49,999 (Silver) to ₹4,99,999 (Royal) and include stage decoration, floral setups, lighting, entry gates, welcome boards, DJ sound, anchors, photography, and more. Each tier builds upon the previous one. We also offer fully custom packages.

Do you provide catering and entertainment services?

Yes, we are a full-service event company. Beyond decoration, we arrange multi-cuisine catering, professional anchors (male & female), live singers, DJ & orchestra, dance groups, dhol-nagara, vintage cars, and even helicopter entries for your grand celebration.
