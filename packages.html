Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance do we need to book a package?

Booking at least 1 month in advance is required (1 month pehle book karna zaroori hai). This guarantees that our planning, decor design, and coordination teams are fully reserved for your dates and can prepare custom layouts to perfection.

Is catering included in your packages?

Catering services are customizable and can be added according to your selected package (Silver, Gold, or Platinum). We customize the catering menu and price structure depending on your total guest count and cuisines selected.

What guest sizes are the packages designed for?

Our packages are segmented by guest sizes: Silver Package is designed for 100–200 Guests, Gold Package is suitable for 200–400 Guests, and Platinum Package is perfect for 300–600 Guests. We also offer bespoke adjustments for larger guest counts.

Can we customize the features in a package?

Yes, absolutely! All packages serve as a baseline. You can add or swap services (such as upgrading stage sizes, adding live music, reel coverage, or specific entrance special effects) according to your preferences.

Need a Custom Package?

Every event is unique. Tell us your vision and budget, and we'll create a tailored package just for you.
